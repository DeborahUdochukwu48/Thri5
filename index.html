Thri5 concept: route the fix to the person on the floor

Thri5 already routes each issue to the person who can fix it. This takes routing one step further: the store manager can pass a physical check to whoever is on shift, and Thri5 still verifies the result against the source data. Try different people and different counts (0, 3 or 27).
